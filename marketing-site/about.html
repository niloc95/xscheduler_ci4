About

Scheduling software that respects your business

WebScheduler was built for service businesses that want a serious booking platform without handing their customer data to someone else.

Booking tools tend to force a trade-off: convenient, but your customers and revenue data sit on a platform you don't control — and every booking chips away at your margin. We thought service businesses deserved better.

WebScheduler gives clinics, salons and multi-provider teams a complete appointment platform — booking pages, calendars, customer records, services and analytics — that runs on your own infrastructure, under your own brand. One flat monthly price, no per-booking fees, no data held hostage.
