Pricing questions

What does "self-hosted" mean for me?

WebScheduler runs on your own server or hosting account. Your appointments, customers and business data live with you — not on a third-party platform.

Are there per-booking fees?

No. You pay one flat monthly price for your plan. Take as many bookings as you like — the price doesn't change.

Can I change plans later?

Yes. Move up as your team grows or down if you need to — changes take effect on your next billing cycle.

Do you offer onboarding help?

Business plans include onboarding assistance. On any plan, our team can help you get set up — just book a demo and we'll walk you through it.
